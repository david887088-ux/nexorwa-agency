Summary

We collect as little personal data as possible. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. Optional services (analytics and maps) only run after you consent, and you can change your choice at any time via .

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the site, the server automatically records technical data such as your IP address, date and time of access, the page requested and browser information. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest). GitHub stores this data in accordance with its own privacy statement. [EDIT: update this section if you do not host on GitHub Pages]

Contact form, email and messengers

When you send us a request via the contact form, we process the data you enter (name, email address, optional phone number or Telegram username, selected service, budget and your message) to answer your request and, where applicable, to prepare an offer (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and on the basis of the consent you give via the checkbox (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

The form is transmitted via the service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your message to our email inbox. If you contact us by email, Telegram or WhatsApp, the respective provider also processes your data under its own privacy policy.

We delete enquiry data once it is no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after our last contact — unless a contract is concluded, in which case statutory retention periods apply.

Cookies and local storage

We store two technically necessary entries in your browser’s local storage: your selected language and your cookie-consent choice. They contain no profile and are not shared. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and § 25(2) TDDDG (strictly necessary). All optional tools described below are only activated with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).

Google Analytics 4 (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Analytics uses cookies (e.g. _ga, _ga_*, stored for up to 2 years) to recognise your browser and helps us understand how the website is used: pages viewed, time on page, approximate region, device and browser type, and clicks on contact buttons. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We have disabled Google signals and advertising personalisation.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, Google Analytics is not loaded at all. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via ; analytics cookies are then deleted. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account and then deleted automatically.

Google Maps (only with your consent)

The map in the “Location” section is provided by Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). It is only loaded after you consent or click “Show map”. Loading the map transmits your IP address and other technical data to Google, which may also set cookies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Further information: Google Privacy Policy.

Fonts and 3D graphics

To display our typography, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). The interactive 3D graphic uses the open-source library three.js, delivered via the jsDelivr content delivery network. When these files are loaded, your IP address is transmitted to the respective provider. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a consistent, fast and secure presentation of the website).

Recipients and international transfers

We share personal data only with the service providers named in this policy. Where data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA (in particular the USA), this is based on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

To exercise your rights, write to [EDIT: email → config.email or config.privacy.privacyEmail]. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your residence or place of work.

Is providing data required?

You are not obliged to provide personal data. Without your contact details, however, we cannot reply to your request. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

Security and changes

This website is delivered over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We update this policy when our services or the legal situation change; the version published here always applies.
